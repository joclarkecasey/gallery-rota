CACM Gallery Rota · proposal for discussion

Fairer gallery and Art Walk hours

Nothing here is live yet. This shows how the rota app and reminder emails would look if we go ahead. The names in the app are examples; the hours are real.

Why change

The gallery is open Thursday to Saturday, 11 to 5, so the number of hours to cover depends on how many of those days fall in the month. A flat 4 hours each doesn’t always cover it.

The idea

  • Each month, the app and emails show how many gallery hours need covering and what that means per artist, with the Art Walk hours shown on their own line so it’s clear they aren’t part of the gallery hours.
  • Gallery hours per artist = that month’s gallery hours ÷ the artists with a minimum (17 at the moment). If it falls between two numbers, it shows a range, such as 4–5.
  • Art Walk is a yearly commitment: 12 Art Walks × 3 people × 3 hours, shared between 17 artists, is about 6 hours each a year.
  • The count of artists updates by itself when someone is added or archived.
  • Reminder emails still go to everyone, with a line asking anyone who hasn’t done or booked their hours to pick some up or arrange a sub. Artists can trade between themselves, for example extra gallery hours in return for someone covering their Art Walk.

What we need to decide
