Booking your gallery hours

Open the rota app

How the hours work

  • Gallery hours. Each month, the app and emails show how many gallery hours need covering and what that means per artist: that month’s gallery hours ÷ the number of artists. If it falls between two numbers, it shows a range, such as 4–5. Months with more open days mean a few more hours each.
  • Art Walk is separate, and yearly. Each Art Walk hour needs 2 people, plus one extra on show-opening nights. That works out to about 3 Art Walk hours each a year. This starts on 1 January 2027; until then the app just shows the Art Walk hours you’ve done.
  • Everyone can see the hours. The Hours tab lists everyone’s gallery hours this month, and from January the year so far and Art Walk too. A few artists have a different arrangement agreed with the gallery; they’re noted at the bottom.
  • Subbing and other events (installs, receptions) are extra. When you sub for someone, the hours count toward their total, not yours.
  1. 1

    Sign in with your email

    Open gallery-rota.vercel.app and type the email address the gallery has for you. There’s no password. Your phone remembers you next time.

    You can also get to the app from the gallery calendar: open any rota entry, such as a grey OPEN – needs cover box, and tap the link in it.

    Tip: add the app to your home screen so it opens like an app.

    Using someone else’s phone or the gallery computer? Tap Change at the top and sign in with your own email. Tap it again when you’re done, so the next person can sign in as themselves.

  2. 2

    Check your month

    The box at the top shows the month’s gallery hours and what that is each, then your own hours: done, booked and still needed. Art Walk is on its own line, with your hours this year (from January, out of about 3). Next month’s hours are always shown too, so you can book ahead. For everyone’s hours, tap the Hours tab.

    Below that, each day shows every hour. Open hours need someone.

  3. 3

    Take open hours

    Tap an open hour, then tap the hours next to it to take a longer block. Check the bar at the bottom, then tap Take.

    It goes straight onto the gallery calendar.

  4. 4

    Sitting for someone else?

    Once you’ve agreed it with them, you can book hours for another artist. Before you tap Take, change For myself to Subbing for and their name.

    Covering hours they’ve already booked? Ask them to give those hours up first, then take them with Subbing for their name.

    The hours count toward their hours, not yours.

  5. 5

    Subbed hours

    On your screen, those squares show a small “for” and the person’s name. Everyone else sees that you’re subbing.

    Either of you can give the hours back if plans change.

  6. 6

    Can’t make it?

    Tap your own hours (green), then Give up. They open up again for someone else.

    Please give hours up as early as you can.

  7. 7

    See just your hours

    Tap Mine to see everything you’ve booked.

  8. 8

    See it on the gallery calendar

    Everything you book in the app appears on the shared gallery calendar by itself.

    Blue = booked, with the artist's name. Grey = OPEN, needs cover. Events such as the Art Walk, installs and receptions have their own colour, and closed days show as a banner across the top.

    The calendar is for viewing only. Tap an OPEN slot and it links you to the app, where you take the hours.

Please book through the app only

The calendar updates by itself from the app, so there’s no need to edit it. Questions? Ask Nadia.
