Booking your gallery hours

Everyone sits at least 4 hours of gallery time a month. Here’s how to book them, sub for someone, or give hours back, all in the rota app.

Open the rota app
  1. 1

    Sign in with your email

    Open gallery-rota.vercel.app and type the email address the gallery has for you. There’s no password. Your phone remembers you next time.

    Tip: add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

  2. 2

    Check your month

    The box at the top shows your hours this month: done, booked, and whether you’ve reached the 4-hour minimum. Gallery sitting and Art Walk both count. Other events (installs, receptions) are extra, and any hours you’ve subbed for others are listed too.

    Below that, each day shows every hour. Open hours need someone.

  3. 3

    Take open hours

    Tap an open hour, then tap the hours next to it to take a longer block. Check the bar at the bottom, then tap Take.

    It goes straight onto the gallery calendar.

  4. 4

    Sitting for someone else?

    Once you’ve agreed it with them, you can book hours for another artist. Before you tap Take, change For myself to Subbing for and their name.

    Covering hours they’ve already booked? Ask them to give those hours up first, then take them with Subbing for their name.

    The hours count toward their 4 hours, not yours.

  5. 5

    Subbed hours

    On your screen, those squares show a small “for” and the person’s name. Everyone else sees that you’re subbing.

    Either of you can give the hours back if plans change.

  6. 6

    Can’t make it?

    Tap your own hours (green), then Give up. They open up again for someone else.

    Please give hours up as early as you can.

  7. 7

    See just your hours

    Tap Mine to see everything you’ve booked.

  8. 8

    See it on the gallery calendar

    Everything you book in the app appears on the shared gallery calendar by itself.

    Blue = booked, with the artist's name. Grey = OPEN, needs cover. Events such as the Art Walk, installs and receptions have their own colour, and closed days show as a banner across the top.

    The calendar is for viewing only. Tap an OPEN slot and it links you to the app, where you take the hours.

Please book through the app only

The calendar updates by itself from the app, so there’s no need to edit it. Questions? Ask Nadia.
